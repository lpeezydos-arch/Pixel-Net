## Priority Issues

**[P2] The net card is wider than the terrain card on upright tablets and narrow windows.**
- Why it matters: 488 vs 375 px at 720×900 and 549 vs 436 px at 768×1024. The spec amendment says the net card is never wider than the terrain; `layout.ts:93` takes `Math.max`. Two centered cards of unequal width read as a misaligned column.
- Fix: in `portraitLayout`'s shared column, make the terrain at least `netSize + 2·inner + gap + READOUT_SIDE` when the readout is beside the net (or move the readout below), then clamp `netCardWidth` to `terrainWidth`. Add 720×844 and 768×968 assertions to `layout.test.ts`.
- Suggested command: /impeccable layout.

**[P2] The row readout's middle column shifts as the value changes.**
- Why it matters: `.net-card[data-readout='below'] .readout` uses `justify-content: space-between`, so "Aspect" moves as its value changes width, under the finger, during a drag.
- Fix: `app.css:486`: drop `space-between`; give each `.stat` `flex: 1 1 0` so the three columns are fixed thirds.
- Suggested command: /impeccable polish.

**[P2] No way to clear a selection on a phone.**
- Why it matters: Escape is the only exit; a tap always selects. The prior fix was keyboard-only and the primary device is the hand.
- Fix: a double-tap on `.terrain__area` clears, calls `onClear` ("Selection cleared"), and the facts caption on touch can end "· Double-tap to clear" the first time. Alternatives: a tap on the ring clears, or the point fades after release.
- Suggested command: /impeccable adapt.

**[P3] The sun's resting label collides with the net.**
- Why it matters: `SunHandle.place` puts the label toward the center, so on the E or W side it overprints "30°"/"60°"; near the center it can run under the highlighted point; at the 180 px net it spans half the net.
- Fix: place the label on the side facing away from the center; below the disc within ~0.3 rim of the center; hide the resting (not the raised) label when `size < 220`, keeping the accessible name.
- Suggested command: /impeccable polish.

**[P3] The first raise of the sun label flashes gray-on-gray.**
- Why it matters: `.sun__label` transitions `color` and `background` between opposite pairs, so for ~50–100 ms the pill is mid-gray with near-invisible text.
- Fix: swap the two forms without transitioning color/background, or fade a separate dark layer's opacity.
- Suggested command: /impeccable animate.

## Persona Red Flags

**Alex (power user):** the sun's tooltip opens the same lesson on every keyboard focus and only a key press dismisses it; no way to type a coordinate or an azimuth; the gray flash on the first key press.

**Sam (screen reader / keyboard):** Tab order and announcements work. Flags: nothing announces that the DEM has loaded or that a retry succeeded; `main[data-status]` changes silently and the caption is not live.

**Casey (one-handed phone, interrupted):** the sun handle and info dot are out of one-handed reach; no thumb gesture clears a selection; a killed tab loses the place she was reading, by spec.

**Priya (hydrologist with a site in mind):** the terrain card carries no north or scale cue; "5 m pixels · 288 × 294" is the only hint of extent; returning tomorrow means re-finding the pixel by eye.

## Minor Observations

- The spec's "one sentence per tooltip" is stale: `NET_HELP` is three sentences, seven lines at 260 px.
- Type does not scale with the cards: at 1920×1080 the net is 665 px, the resting sun label 12 px, the readout 18 px.
- The error sentence names the problem but not the cause; "Check your connection" would help a first visit with no signal.
- The touch stir is real but subtle enough to miss.
- The selection ring at column 0 draws half outside the card; visible, not clipped.
- Cognitive load: 0 failures of 8.
- Not captured: real drag latency and spring feel, safe-area insets, Safari/Firefox.

## Questions to Consider

1. Should the sun's resting position live on the rim as a tick at its azimuth, so the cloud is never overprinted and the label never meets "30°"?
2. What is the touch idiom for "back to the portrait": double-tap, a tap on the point itself, or should the net point fade after release and the caption's facts be the only trace?
3. Could the point on the net carry its own tiny label so the eye never leaves the net while dragging, with the row demoted to the resting state?
4. Is a quiet north and scale cue on the terrain a map control, or the one thing standing between Priya and "no GIS"?
5. When a selection exists, should the hint line say how to leave it, the way the sun says "Double-tap to reset"?
